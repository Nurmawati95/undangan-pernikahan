## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the regal elegance, warmth, and ritual grandeur of high-end Indonesian bridal celebrations. It bridges traditional archipelago hospitality with high-fashion couture publishing. 

The aesthetic blends **Romantic Glassmorphism** with **Regal Editorial Print**:
- **Atmospheric Depth:** Frosted ivory and warm champagne glass overlays soften vibrant photography and gold leaf ornamentation.
- **Architectural Motifs:** Arched window frames, bespoke Islamic and traditional Indonesian symmetry (such as *Kain Songket* and *Batik Kawung* line geometry interpreted via gossamer hairline strokes), and warm metallic framing.
- **Poise & Intimacy:** The interface evokes the ceremonial cadence of bespoke stationery, preserving reverence while delivering fluid digital touchpoints for RSVPs, music curation, live stream attendance, and digital envelopes (*Angpao*).

## Colors

The palette draws directly from royal wedding attire, ceremonial batik pigments, and gilded court invitations:

- **Primary (`#FF3E9B` / Deep Romantic Rose):** Represents passionate union and celebratory festivity; used for primary celebration call-to-actions, accent seals, and focal points. Paired with soft blush (`#FF88BA`) for delicate glow backgrounds and interactive states.
- **Secondary (`#3A8B95` / Rich Peacock Teal):** Infuses heritage majesty, balance, and quiet poise. Supported by seafoam cyan (`#66D0BC`) for subtle tints, nature motifs, and calm informational tags.
- **Tertiary (`#D4AF37` / Champagne Gold):** Emulates gold thread (*benang emas*), foil stamping, and heirloom jewelry. Accompanied by antique brass (`#C5A059`) for tactile hairline borders, ornamental dividers, and monograms.
- **Neutral (`#1F1916` / Rich Espresso):** Grounding editorial contrast for all readable typography, providing a softer, more poetic presence than stark black.
- **Surface Foundations:** Warm Ivory Cream (`#FDFBF7`) serves as the core canvas, layered with sheer frosted whites (`rgba(253, 251, 247, 0.72)`) for luminous glass cards.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy harmonizes the formal cadence of high-end wedding invitations:

- **Playfair Display:** Commands all matrimonial titular hierarchies, couple names, and chapter titles. The italic variant should be applied strategically to transitional words (e.g., *"The Wedding of"*, *"and"*, *"Undangan Pernikahan"*).
- **Plus Jakarta Sans:** A contemporary Indonesian grotesque font that delivers legibility for event details, schedules (Akad Nikah & Resepsi), maps, live guestbooks, and interactive forms.
- **Uppercase Tracking:** All `label-gold` tokens must be styled in uppercase with generous letter spacing to evoke gold foil stationery debossing.

## Layout & Spacing

Digital wedding invitations are inherently mobile-first, crafted for guests receiving invitations via instant messaging:

- **Grid System:** Single-column fluid canvas on mobile (`max-width: 480px`) centered gracefully on wider viewports with a subtle framed presentation. Desktop layouts utilize a 12-column layout maxing out at `1120px` for multi-column gallery showcases and registry management.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Generous vertical intervals (`space-xl` to `4rem`) demarcate ceremonial acts (Cover, Couple Bio, Event Details, RSVP, Digital Envelope, Wishes).
- **Arched Frame Constraints:** Featured portrait imagery and ceremony cards observe an aspect ratio inspired by architectural portals (typically 4:5 or 9:16) with top-aligned organic curves.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy evokes layered handmade vellum paper, warm frosted acrylic, and gilded metallic underlays:

- **Surface Tiers & Frost:**
  - Base: Warm Ivory Cream `#FDFBF7`.
  - Mid-Layer Glass: `rgba(253, 251, 247, 0.78)` backed by `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and an inner hairline stroke of `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.35)`.
  - Floating Overlays (Modals, QR Codes): `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)` with warm ambient shadowing.
- **Ambient Shadow Character:** Shadows avoid cool gray tones; they are warm, diffused, and tinted by the neutral espresso tone:
  - Ambient Card: `0 12px 36px -8px rgba(31, 25, 22, 0.08), 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.12)`.
  - Floating Action / Seal: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(255, 62, 155, 0.28)`.
- **Gold Foil Outlines:** Glass surfaces use a dual border trick: a 1px solid border at `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.4)` and an interior inset box shadow of `inset 0 0 0 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`.

## Shapes

The form language balances structural poise with romantic arches:

- **Default Corners:** Core cards, text inputs, and modal wrappers use `0.5rem` (`roundedness: 2`), reflecting the subtle cut of heavy-stock wedding stationery.
- **Architectural Dome/Arch Cut:** Hero frames, photo portraits, and major chapter banners adopt asymmetric rounded profiles: top corners styled with `rounded-t-[120px]` or `rounded-t-full`, leaving bottom corners at standard card radii.
- **Seals and Ornamental Buttons:** Wax seals, audio toggles, and circular monograms are strictly rounded to full circles (`rounded-full`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Ceremonial Primary (Open Invitation, RSVP Confirmation):** Rich Rose background (`#FF3E9B`) transitioning to deep blush on hover, high-contrast white text (`#FFFFFF`), subtle gold rim (`border: 1px solid #D4AF37`), and gentle glow shadow.
- **Secondary / Gilded Ghost:** Ivory glass fill with crisp antique gold border (`#C5A059`), typography set in Rich Espresso (`#1F1916`) with `label-gold` tracking.
- **Floating Floating Audio Controller:** Circular translucent button fixed to the viewport corner, featuring frosted teal tint (`rgba(58, 139, 149, 0.25)`), gold glyph, and slow rotational pulse animation.

### Cards & Ceremony Modules
- **Event Timeline Cards (Akad & Resepsi):** Translucent ivory glass panels framed in arched top profiles. Include date badges with champagne gold borders, clear add-to-calendar triggers, and directional map buttons.
- **Love Story & Gallery Cards:** Framed within hairline gold filigree borders, overlaid with soft vignette gradients for photography captions.

### Inputs & Guestbook Forms
- **Fields:** Warm, bone-white backgrounds (`#FFFFFF`) with bottom-bordered or softly framed borders (`#D4AF37` at 40% opacity). Focus states shift the border to rich peacock teal (`#3A8B95`) with an ambient outer glow.
- **Text & Placeholder:** Neutral espresso text with muted champagne placeholder indicators.

### Checkboxes & Attendance Radios
- Custom circular indicators mimicking wax stamps: inactive states feature an antique gold hairline border; active states display a solid rose center stamped with an ivory checkmark.

### Bespoke Matrimonial Components
- **Digital Envelope & Gift Modal (*Amplop Digital*):** Bank account cards styled as polished credit metal (champagne gold or peacock teal finishes) with single-tap "Copy Account Number" toast confirmation and verified badge.
- **Wax Seal Cover Gate:** Modal cover overlay displaying a ceremonial 3D gold monogram wax seal requiring a guest tap to unlock and reveal the invitation with an audio swell.
- **Live Stream Indicator:** Pill badge combining seafoam cyan pulse dot with deep peacock teal typography to indicate active ceremonial broadcasting.
